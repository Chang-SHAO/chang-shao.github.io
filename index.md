I recently completed my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence at the [School of Computer Science](https://www.uts.edu.au/about/faculties/engineering-and-information-technology/computer-science), [University of Technology Sydney (UTS)](https://www.uts.edu.au/), affiliated with the [Australian Artificial Intelligence Institute (AAII)](https://www.uts.edu.au/research/centres/australian-artificial-intelligence-institute). I was fortunate to be supervised by A/Prof. [Jing Jiang](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Jing.Jiang), with Chair Prof. [Chengqi Zhang](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Chengqi.Zhang) (IEEE Fellow) and Chair Prof. [Yuhui Shi](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/faculties/shiyuhui.html) (IEEE Fellow) as co-supervisors. My doctoral research focused on **dynamic multi-objective optimization: benchmarks and algorithms**.

My research lies at the intersection of **evolutionary computation** and **machine learning**, with a focus on optimization problems whose objectives, constraints, or environments change over time. I am particularly interested in **dynamic multi-objective optimization**, **LLM-assisted dynamic optimization**, and the **dynamic evolution and adaptation of agents**.

I am one of the three core developers of [pypop](https://github.com/Evolutionary-Intelligence/pypop), a pure-Python library for large-scale black-box optimization (~290 GitHub stars, 150k+ downloads); related work was published as a co-first-authored paper in the **Journal of Machine Learning Research**. I also lead the development of [pydmoo](https://github.com/dynoptimization/pydmoo), a pure-Python library for dynamic multi-objective optimization, and have contributed bug fixes to [pymoo](https://github.com/the-dual-space/pymoo). My work has appeared in **Applied Soft Computing**, **Swarm and Evolutionary Computation**, **Journal of Machine Learning Research**, **IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems**, and conferences such as **PPSN**, **GECCO**, **CEC**, and **EMO**. My full biography is [here](./biography/).
